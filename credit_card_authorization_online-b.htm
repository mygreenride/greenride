Payment & Credit Card Policy

New customers: The physical credit card and cardholder must be present at pickup. GreenRide does not accept remote, telephone, or emailed credit card authorizations for first-time customers. Payment may be made with cash or by presenting the physical card in person.

Returning customers: Remote credit card authorization is available for repeat customers in good standing. Additional verification may be required.

These procedures help protect customers, drivers, and GreenRide against unauthorized card use.
