How These Rates Were Modernized

2026 update method: the historical 2017 rate was increased by 40%, then rounded up to the next $5. This produces simple whole-dollar pricing with no pennies.

Example: the historical $194 LAX rate becomes $271.60 after a 40% increase, then rounds up to $275.
