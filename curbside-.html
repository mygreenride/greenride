2 Phone Call Policy

1st Call

  • Call us as soon as your plane lands.
  • Call 800-893-7210 or 619-200-2060 before you exit the plane.

2nd Call

  • Call again when you are outside at the main exit by the curb.
  • Find the nearest zone sign such as A, B, C, or another posted letter, and tell us your zone.

After your second call, your driver will pull up to your location.

Find the Nearest Zone Sign

When you reach the curb, look for the nearest lettered zone sign and have the zone ready when you call.

Important Notes

  • All tolls and parking fees are additional.
  • 20% gratuity will be added to all reservations.
  • Additional waiting time charges, if applicable, are charged by the minute.
  • Up to 4 passengers from the same party and pickup address ride for the same sedan rate.
  • GreenRide.net may charge for time spent outside normal point-to-point service at $80 per hour plus 20% gratuity.
