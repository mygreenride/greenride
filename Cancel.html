Cancellation Policy

GreenRide San Diego Transportation

Call: 619-200-2060 Toll-Free: 800-893-7210

No refunds are available for services already rendered.

How to Cancel — Email Only

Phone calls are not accepted for cancellations.

Send your cancellation request from the same email address used for the reservation or the associated credit-card authorization.

Cancellation@GreenRide.net

Please include the reservation date, pickup time, pickup address, destination, passenger phone number, and reason for cancellation.

Refunds & Timing

  • A full refund is available when the cancellation email is received at least 4 hours before pickup during Green Ride business hours.
  • Refunds normally appear within approximately 2 business days, depending on the card issuer and bank.
  • No refund is available for cancellations received with less than 4 hours' qualifying notice or for no-shows.

Rates & Online Reservations

Reservation rates are based on the rates published on the Green Ride Airport Rates page.

Customers are responsible for reviewing the applicable rate before submitting a reservation.

If a different amount is entered by the customer, that amount does not replace the published Green Ride rate. By submitting a reservation, the customer agrees to GreenRide.net policies and terms.

Meet & Greet

For Meet & Greet airport service, the driver will meet the passenger near the baggage claim carousel area.

If you cannot locate your driver, call 800-893-7210 .

Curbside Pickup

For curbside pickup at San Diego International Airport, Green Ride uses a two-call procedure.

  1. Call 619-200-2060 or 800-893-7210 when your flight arrives and phone use is permitted.
  2. Call again after collecting your luggage and reaching the curb.

This procedure allows the driver to approach the terminal when the passenger is ready and helps reduce unnecessary airport congestion.

Travel Time & Airport Arrival

Airlines commonly recommend arriving at the airport approximately two hours before departure. Green Ride recommends allowing adequate time for traffic, airport congestion, security screening, and other possible delays.

Transportation operates on public roadways and may be affected by traffic, weather, road closures, accidents, airport conditions, and other circumstances beyond Green Ride's control.

For that reason, arrival at a particular destination or airport at an exact time cannot be guaranteed. Green Ride will make every reasonable effort to provide timely transportation.
