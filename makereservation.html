Why We Require a Phone Call

Accurate Flight & Pickup Details

Airline schedules and curbside logistics can change quickly. A brief telephone conversation allows Green Ride to confirm your terminal, flight information, curbside pickup location, luggage details, and other transportation needs.

Safety & Reliability

Phone verification helps reduce booking errors and helps make sure the driver receives the correct passenger, pickup, destination, and timing information.
