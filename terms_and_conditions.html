Airport Curbside Pickup Policy

First call: Call when your plane lands before exiting the aircraft. Second call: Call again when you are outside the main exit doors at the curb. Find the nearest zone sign and give us your location. Your driver will approach after the second call.

Vehicles are not permitted to wait at the curb except while actively loading or unloading passengers.

Cancellation Policy

Cancellations are accepted by email only and are valid once acknowledged by Green Ride. Minimum notice for the San Diego area is 4 hours prior to pickup, although restrictions may apply.

Other regions may require between 4 and 24 hours notice. Europe, Central America, and South America may require 24 hours notice. Far East and Australia may require 48 hours notice.

Late cancellations may be billed at the prevailing minimum hourly rate according to market and vehicle type. Obtain a cancellation number or acknowledgement to avoid late cancellation charges.
